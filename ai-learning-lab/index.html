Connecting AI and Software Engineering

The behavior of an AI application depends on more than the model itself. Data handling, API design, evaluation, latency, monitoring, and failure recovery all influence the reliability of the complete system.

This technical focus examines those connections through engineering analysis, practical implementations, and evidence-based evaluation.
